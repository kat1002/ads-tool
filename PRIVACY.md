# Playable Batch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Playable Batch is a browser extension that helps ad-playable creators process playable ad files in batch. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

## Summary

Playable Batch does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data to the developer. The developer operates no server and receives no analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage data.

## What the extension does with data

- **Store links you paste.** A Google Play or Apple App Store link is used only to request the public app page or lookup data from the store that owns the link, and to download the app icon from that store's image host (`play.google.com`, `itunes.apple.com`, `*.googleusercontent.com`, `*.mzstatic.com`). These requests go directly from your browser to those stores. Cookies are not sent. The stores' own privacy policies apply to those requests.
- **Playable files you load.** ZIP and HTML files are processed locally in your browser. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Notes and settings.** Link notes, categories and tool settings are saved only in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. They are not synced and not sent to the developer. Clear the extension's data or uninstall the extension to remove them.

## What the extension does not do

- No collection of personal information, browsing history or page content.
- No tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, third-party SDKs or analytics.
- No remote code: all scripts are bundled in the extension package.
- No sale or transfer of data to third parties.

## Permissions

- `storage`: keeps your notes and settings on your device.
- Host access to Google Play, the Apple lookup service and their image hosts: reads the public name and icon for links you paste.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
